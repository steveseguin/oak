Your radio. Your browser.

The website can point you to a radio client without running a public gateway or sharing anyone's private messages.

MeshCore web client

Connect a supported companion radio using Bluetooth or USB Serial. The client runs in your browser and asks you to choose your device.

Open MeshCore web client

External MeshCore application. Browser and device support varies; desktop Chrome or Edge is a good starting point. A repeater such as Oak Roof is not an ordinary chat companion.

Oak Mesh local app

Already running Oak Mesh on this computer? Open its local chat and collector. It keeps listening through its local service when its browser tab closes.

Open my local Oak Mesh

Only works if you have the local app installed and running. This address means your own computer, not a shared Meshcast server. This site does not connect to or read your local app.

One radio connection at a time

Disconnect a companion from its current phone app or collector before connecting it to another client. Keep the client open while using browser Bluetooth or Serial. Use the local collector when you need persistent background listening.
